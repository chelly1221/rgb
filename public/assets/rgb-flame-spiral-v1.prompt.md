## Final prompt

Create one minimalist app icon on a genuinely transparent alpha background, delivered as RGBA PNG. The emblem is EXACTLY THREE stylized abstract flame forms of different sizes that PARTIALLY OVERLAP AND WRAP AROUND ONE SHARED VERTICAL AXIS, spiraling upward together. Think three broad smooth flame-shaped ribbons winding around each other in a rising helix: the lowest small flame sweeps across the front, the medium flame curls from behind and crosses the central area, and the tallest flame continues the shared spiral to an upward taper. The three pieces should visibly interleave with subtle front/behind overlap, making ONE compact cohesive spiral silhouette. Their curves flow in the SAME rotational direction around the common axis. NOT three separate upright wavy columns, NOT side-by-side flames, NOT a trident. Keep each flame recognizable through a small overlap boundary and its different tip height, while the whole reads as an ascending spiral. Broad simple shapes, smooth elegant curves, rounded organic lower contours, very few details, readable as a Windows taskbar icon. Flat vector-like graphic, gentle warm gradients of pink, vermilion, orange and golden yellow across the three forms; subtle tonal separation at overlaps, no outlines and no complex 3D shading. No literal fire, smoke, sparks, detached dots, glossy material, glow, shadow, text, badge, container, or background. Center the emblem on a square canvas with generous transparent margins. TRUE TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND: empty alpha outside the emblem, do not paint checkerboard pixels.
